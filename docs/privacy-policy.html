Privacy Policy

Aeonfall: Storyboard RPG · Effective 25 August 2026

This policy explains how F7 Developer (“we”, “us”) handles information in Aeonfall: Storyboard RPG (“the app”). We designed the app to work without an account and without uploading your gameplay or voice recordings.

Information stored on your device

The app stores gameplay progress, run history, accessibility choices, audio preferences, and the ad-free entitlement locally on your device. This is used to resume the game and remember your settings. We do not operate a cloud save or user account for Aeonfall.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show optional banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertising. Rewarded ads play only if you choose to watch them. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interaction, and diagnostic information to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve advertising. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

On supported devices, Google’s User Messaging Platform presents consent choices where required. You can revisit privacy choices from the app’s privacy/consent controls when available. We do not sell your personal information.

Purchases

The app offers a one-time “Remove Ads” purchase through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment, billing credentials, tax, and transaction processing. The app receives purchase status from Google Play so it can grant or restore the ad-free entitlement. We do not receive or store your payment card number.

Audio, camera, and microphone

Aeonfall plays bundled music, effects, and narration. It does not record, upload, or transcribe microphone audio, and it does not request camera access.

Children

Aeonfall is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app’s target audience and advertising configuration are set accordingly in Google Play.

Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Google retains advertising and purchase information under its own policies and controls. For Google-held information, use the controls available in your Google account, Google Play, or Google’s privacy resources.

Security

We limit the app’s own collection to what is necessary for local gameplay and the optional services described above. No network service can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to keep the app and its hosted policy information maintained.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, laws, or third-party services change. The effective date above will be updated with the next revision.
