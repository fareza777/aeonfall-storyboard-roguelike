# Aeonfall 2.2.6 (18)

## Player-facing release notes

- Improved damage previews for Ward, difficulty scaling, special attacks and Footnote.
- Reward offers and collected items now survive save/resume without duplicate claims.
- The Archive now grants its promised extra card choice, including with Marrow Die and Ascension.

## Scope and verification

This update addresses findings 1-3 in the audit summary (damage forecasts,
duplicate rewards and Archive). It does not change artwork, ad placements,
purchase pricing or the existing 178-country production distribution.

The three fixes passed 133 repository tests after the review correction pass. An
existing test warning about an offscreen Act II BEGIN tap is unrelated to
the production changes. Static analysis reports no issues. The fresh review
identified three additional forecast boundaries (Mirror strikes, expiring hero
modifiers and Bleed stopping multi-hit foes); execution-versus-preview tests
first reproduced them and now pass. Unequal-hit labels are no longer averaged.

The final release AAB was rebuilt after those changes and validated with
Google's [bundletool 1.18.3](https://github.com/google/bundletool/releases/tag/1.18.3).
Its manifest is `com.aeonfall.game`, version `2.2.6`, code `18`, target SDK `36`.
Jarsigner verifies its signature. Its existing self-signed Android upload
certificate produces trust/timestamp and ZIP streaming warnings; the Android
bundle validator succeeds. Google Play's upload validation is checked before
submission rather than changing or bypassing signing.

Final bundle: `build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`, 107,460,992 bytes.
SHA-256: `0fcd078588f13e085e7170b5acb27737fb158115eaf867967532d6893ce6a960`.
Upload certificate SHA-256: `40:BD:07:4B:69:59:48:D8:AB:B9:3F:12:D8:3B:96:A3:AC:08:D4:A7:D2:EB:FD:16:46:09:33:70:9A:7D:AE:D4`.
Console submission and remote-main outcomes are recorded once verified.

Metadata and old run saves remain readable. New reward sessions persist their
offers and six claim flags inside the run save. A resolved boss checkpoint
remains resumable through the Act/finale transition.

Older saves never recorded claims made by the previous version, so a past
claim cannot be reconstructed retroactively; this update does not deduct
currency or erase progress to guess that history.

Restore tests verify serialized checkpoints and claim idempotency. No abrupt
physical-device power-loss test was performed; unrelated audit findings were
not included in this targeted patch.
