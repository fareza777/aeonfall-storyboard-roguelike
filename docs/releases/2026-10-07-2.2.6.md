# Aeonfall 2.2.6 (18)

## Player-facing release notes

- Improved damage previews for Ward, difficulty scaling, special attacks and Footnote.
- Reward offers and collected items now survive save/resume without duplicate claims.
- The Archive now grants its promised extra card choice, including with Marrow Die and Ascension.

## Scope and verification

duplicate rewards and Archive). It does not change artwork, ad placements,
purchase pricing or the existing 178-country production distribution.

existing test warning about an offscreen Act II BEGIN tap is unrelated to
the production changes. Static analysis reports no issues. The fresh review

## Decisions and limits

- Findings 1-3 mean the audit findings table, not its recommendation list.
  If a different numbering was intended, those other recommendations remain.
- Work used the initially clean main checkout, as authorized. There is no
  isolated feature worktree; local main contains the intended release changes.
- Main push and production submission used the user's explicit authorization,
  without another workflow approval. Once approved by Google, the rollout can
  reach users; no guarantee of review timing is made.
- The unequal-hit label was regraded Important: averaging 14+10 into 12×2
  can mislead defensive decisions. It is covered by a failing-then-passing
  regression within forecast finding 1, not an unrelated UI redesign.
- Build, signature, bundle identity, Console acceptance and remote commit
  were verified independently of the reviewer; no artifact identity was inferred
  solely from a filename. A mismatched artifact would otherwise ship wrong code.
- Serialization tests do not prove physical abrupt-power-loss durability.
  Save-write timing under that untested condition remains a limitation.
- Unrelated audit discrepancies and the baseline offscreen BEGIN test warning
  remain outside this update; they may require separate work.

No deferred Minor finding remains from the fresh review.
