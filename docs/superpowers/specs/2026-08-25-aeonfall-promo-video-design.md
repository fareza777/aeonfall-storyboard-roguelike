# Aeonfall Promo Video Design

## Format

- 1920x1080 landscape, 30 fps, approximately 50 seconds.
- Remotion scene composition with local Cinzel and Inter fonts.
- Existing `vo_trailer.mp3` is the narration anchor. Existing game art is used
  for visual truth; no invented gameplay UI is presented as real.
- Music is ducked below narration and ads are not shown.

## Story beats

1. The fall and the drawn world.
2. Choose a Vessel and shape a deck.
3. Read the branching map.
4. Battle with Frames and elemental reactions.
5. Follow chronicles and companions.
6. Reach an ending, then begin another draft.

## Distribution

The rendered MP4 is uploaded to the FluxLab YouTube channel as an unlisted,
non-monetized video and linked from the Play listing.
