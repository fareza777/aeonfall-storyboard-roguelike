# Aeonfall Store Assets and Policy Design

## Identity

- Store title: `Aeonfall: Storyboard RPG`
- Package: `com.aeonfall.game`
- Default listing language: English (United States)
- Category: Games / Strategy
- Tags: Card, Roguelike, Fantasy

## Listing copy

Copy uses narrative deckbuilding and roguelite search terms naturally. It does
not claim rankings, awards, prices, or guaranteed outcomes. The listing calls
out offline play, six Vessels, Frames, elemental reactions, branching routes,
companions, multiple endings, and accessibility settings.

## Asset rules

- Icon: 512x512, 32-bit PNG with alpha, under 1 MB.
- Feature graphic: 1024x500, JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha.
- Eight 1080x1920 portrait screenshots from real app captures, composited with
  small narrative captions and no fingers, device frames, pricing, or ranking
  claims.
- The Android immersive-mode overlay is excluded from the listing assets.

## Hosting

GitHub Pages from `docs/` hosts the privacy policy and `app-ads.txt` at the
public repository URL. The same root URL is used as the Play support website.
